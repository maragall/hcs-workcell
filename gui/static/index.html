Teach

One list. Move the arm by hand to where each row says, press Record. The path you dragged becomes the route; the camera takes its picture at the same time. Marker rows let the camera correct small shifts later; they use the marker tab from the kit.

1
  the jaw closes, then the arm goes loose. Press it again to lock the arm.
2
Go down the list. Each row: put the arm where it says, press Record.
rowput the armrecorded
3
  the arm repeats every route at low speed while you watch. STOP at any time.
Done when no row is red. Red rows are exactly what Run will refuse on.

Test

Before anything runs on its own. This is the only place you tell the program where plates are.

1
Click the slots that hold a plate.
2
The arm looks at every slot and compares with your clicks, checks the marker at every taught position, drives the path slowly between the microscope and the rotation module, turns the module, tries a taken slot on purpose and backs out, then takes every plate to the microscope and back in turn, round after round, until you press STOP.
